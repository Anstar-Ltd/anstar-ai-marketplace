# Anstar Sales Help

Use this read-only orientation when a seller asks what the plugin can do or how to begin.

Anstar Sales CRM turns accessible Dataverse account, opportunity, contact, and activity evidence into practical preparation and priorities. It can:

- explain recent signals for one account or a bounded account set;
- rank existing accounts that need attention and show why;
- prepare a CRM-backed brief for a named meeting or account;
- review open pipeline risks and priorities for a defined week;
- answer other bounded, free-form CRM research questions.

Useful prompts:

- “What changed for Contoso in the last 14 days?”
- “Which of my open accounts should I focus on this week?”
- “Prepare me for my meeting with Contoso.”
- “Review my open pipeline for the previous seven days.”

The plugin reads only data available to the signed-in user through `anstar-dataverse`. It does not update CRM, send messages, discover meetings from a calendar, or fill missing evidence with guesses. Ask the user to choose one example only when their request is still broad.
